Common questions

I bought Remove Ads but I still see ads. Open the game → Settings → Restore. The optional "Keep going" ad after you die always stays, since you choose whether to watch it.

I got a new phone. Sign in with the same Apple ID and tap Restore in Settings. Armory upgrades and cash are saved on the phone itself, so they start fresh on a new device.

The leaderboard doesn't show. Sign in to Game Center in your iPhone's Settings app, then reopen the game.

How do I change ad privacy choices? In the EU and UK: game Settings → Ad privacy choices. Everywhere: iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
