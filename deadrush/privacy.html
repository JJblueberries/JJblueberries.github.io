Dead Rush Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Dead Rush ("the game") is a free game supported by ads. This policy explains what data is collected when you play.

What the game itself stores

Your Armory upgrades, cash, best score, sound setting and whether you bought Remove Ads are saved only on your device. We do not run our own servers and do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos or location.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location derived from it, and how you interact with ads. On iPhone you choose whether apps may track you (the "Allow tracking" prompt); if you decline, you still see ads, just less personalized. In the EU, UK and similar regions you are asked for consent first and can change it any time in the game's Settings, under "Ad privacy choices". Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Leaderboards (Apple Game Center)

On iPhone, if you are signed in to Game Center, your score and Game Center nickname are sent to Apple to show leaderboards. Apple's privacy policy: apple.com/legal/privacy

Purchases

"Remove Ads" is purchased through Apple or Google. They process the payment; we never see your card or billing details.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything the game stored on your device. For ad data, use your phone's ad settings or the game's "Ad privacy choices" option.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
