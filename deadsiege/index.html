Common questions

I bought Remove Ads but still see ads between waves. Open the game menu (☰) and tap Restore purchases. Reward ads you choose to watch (double gold, Overdrive, bonus chest) always stay available.

I got a new phone. Sign in with the same Apple ID, open the menu and tap Restore purchases. Your base is saved on the device, so a new phone starts a new base.

Did I lose gold while the app was closed? No. Gold mines keep digging for up to 8 hours. Collect it when you come back.

The leaderboard doesn't show. Sign in to Game Center in your iPhone's Settings app, then reopen the game.

How do I change ad privacy choices? In the EU and UK: game menu → Ad privacy choices. Everywhere: iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
