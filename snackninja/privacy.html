Snack Ninja Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Snack Ninja ("the app") is made by Dylan Wilkins. This policy explains what information is collected when you play.

Information we collect

We do not ask for your name, email, location or any account. Your game progress (stars, unlocked pajamas, streaks and settings) is saved only on your device.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect your device's advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking), an approximate location based on your IP address, and information about how you interact with ads and the app. Google uses this to show ads, measure them, and prevent fraud. You can turn off tracking at any time in iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Players in the EU and UK can change ad choices in the app's Settings. Learn more at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. We never see or store your payment details.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, your scores are sent to Apple's leaderboards and shown with your Game Center name. This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Crash and performance data

Basic crash and performance data may be collected through Apple and Google to fix bugs. It is not used to identify you.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
