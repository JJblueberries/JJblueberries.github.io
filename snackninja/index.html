Common questions

How do I play? Tap next to your kid to take one step. Wait for the ring around them to turn green between taps. Quick taps are loud and wake people up.

I bought Remove Ads but still see ads between nights. Open Settings in the game and tap Restore purchases. Reward videos you choose to watch (show the secret candy, rewind 5 seconds) always stay available.

I got a new phone. Sign in with the same Apple ID, open Settings and tap Restore purchases. Your stars and streak are saved on the device, so a new phone starts fresh.

The leaderboard doesn't show. Sign in to Game Center in your iPhone's Settings app, then reopen the game.

How do I change ad privacy choices? In the EU and UK: game Settings → Ad privacy choices. Everywhere: iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
