Common questions

I bought Remove Ads but I still see ads. Open the game → Settings → Restore purchases. The optional "Watch ad for +2 warheads" button always stays, since you choose whether to use it.

I got a new phone. Sign in with the same Apple ID and tap Restore purchases in Settings.

The leaderboard doesn't show. Sign in to Game Center in your iPhone's Settings app, then reopen the game.

How do I change ad privacy choices? In the EU and UK: game Settings → Ad privacy choices. Everywhere: iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
